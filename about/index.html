About

I build AI systems end to end: agent loops with real tools, retrieval I can measure, models that run on the device, and the product people actually touch.

I’m Sandeep (Sai Sandeep Kumar Vijayarao), an MS CS student at Northeastern University in San Jose, graduating May 2027. I like the part of AI engineering where a model has to survive contact with a real product.

At Trominosoft I built the orchestration, retrieval and tool-use layers of a multi-model platform, load-tested to 10K+ requests a day, then cut its latency 30% and its hallucination rate 38%. At Northeastern I TA three graduate courses, Computer Vision, Generative AI and HCI, and build the evaluation harnesses behind them.

In 2026 I won the Apple Swift Student Challenge with Anu, an iOS agent that runs Gemma 4 entirely on the phone. Building on that stack led me upstream: I now send fixes to LiteRT, MLX Swift, Hugging Face Diffusers and RunAnywhere when the tools I depend on break.
