Day Kit: Privacy Policy and Terms

Day Kit (com.daykit.app.ai), the on-device AI build of Daykit for Android. Version 1.6.0. Last updated 29 October 2026.

The short version

Everything you keep in Day Kit (tasks, notes, lists, bills, spending, countdowns and the rest) is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. There are no Day Kit accounts, no Day Kit servers, and no analytics, ads or trackers.

The AI runs on your phone. Day Kit's only network request is downloading the optional Gemma model, when you ask for it. The Gemini app can reach Day Kit only if you connect it yourself (see below).

Gemini Nano, on your phone

  • "Add anything" and "Plan with Gemini Nano". On phones that have it, what you type is read by Gemini Nano through Android's AICore system service, which runs the model on the device. On other phones, built-in rules read it instead. Day Kit makes no network request for either. Nothing is saved until you tap Save.
  • Calendar. If you allow it, "Plan with Gemini Nano" gives Nano the titles and times of your next seven days of events, so it can pick a free time. Day Kit asks only the first time you ask it to schedule something. The events are read on the phone and never stored or sent. Day Kit works without calendar access.
  • Voice typing. Audio is transcribed on the phone and the recording is deleted straight away.

Hand-off and the accessibility service

Hand-off is off until you switch on "Day Kit hand-off" in Android's accessibility settings. Day Kit first shows you what it reads and asks you to agree. After that, when you tap the accessibility button or the "Hand off" shortcut, Day Kit reads the text on the screen of the app in front, once.

  • It asks Android for no accessibility events, so nothing is read in the background.
  • Password fields are never read.
  • The text is held in memory only while the hand-off sheet is open, and is used on the phone to suggest tasks, bills and notes.
  • It isn't sent anywhere, logged or stored. Only an item you choose to save is kept.

The Gemini app (AppFunctions)

Day Kit offers four functions to Android's AppFunctions system: add a task, add a note, show what's due on a day, and search what you saved. Only system-trusted agents such as Google's Gemini app can call them, and only after you connect Day Kit in that app and mention it ("@Day Kit …"). The agent then receives the result it asked for, such as today's tasks, and handles it under Google's Privacy Policy. Writes are confirmed with you first, and a tool you have switched off is never read or written.

The one network request

Settings → Assistant offers an optional Gemma model. If you tap Install, the model file is downloaded from Google's public host on Hugging Face. The request carries no account, identifier or content of yours. It never happens in the background, and nothing is uploaded.

Other apps

The DoorDash button opens DoorDash's search with the items on a list Gemini Nano suggested. You choose the store and place any order yourself, in DoorDash, under DoorDash's policies. "Add to calendar" opens your calendar app for you to confirm. Day Kit never buys anything, sends messages or signs in on your behalf.

Deleting your data

Each tool can delete its data from its own settings, Settings → Assistant removes the downloaded model, and uninstalling Day Kit removes everything from the phone. We never receive a copy of your Day Kit data, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

Children

Day Kit is a general-purpose productivity app and is not directed at children.

Terms of use

  • Day Kit is provided as is, without warranty of any kind.
  • AI suggestions can be wrong. Check each item before you save it, and check anything you order or schedule in another app.
  • Don't rely on Day Kit for emergencies, or for medical, legal or financial decisions.
  • Use of the Gemini app, DoorDash and your calendar app is also subject to their own terms.
