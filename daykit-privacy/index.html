Day Kit: Privacy Policy and Terms

Day Kit (com.adshackathon.team04), the AI build of Daykit for Android. A prototype built at Android Dev Summit 2026. Last updated 29 October 2026.

The short version

Everything you keep in Day Kit (tasks, notes, lists, bills, spending, countdowns and the rest) is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. There are no Day Kit accounts, no Day Kit servers, and no analytics, ads or trackers.

A few features send data to Google, and only when you use them: "Ask Gemini (cloud)", the Gemini app connection, and the optional model download. Each is described below.

On your phone only

  • "Add anything". What you type is read on the phone, by Gemini Nano through Android's AICore where available, or by built-in rules. Nothing is saved unless you tap Save.
  • Voice typing. Audio is transcribed on the phone and the recording is deleted straight away.
  • Hand-off. When you switch on the "Day Kit hand-off" accessibility service and then tap the accessibility button or the "Hand off" shortcut, Day Kit reads the text on the screen once. The service receives no events in between, so nothing is read in the background, and password fields are never read. The screen text is held only while the hand-off sheet is open. Only what you choose to save is kept, as an ordinary task, note or other item.

What is sent to Google, and when

  • "Ask Gemini (cloud)". Only when you tap this button, Day Kit sends your request to Google's Gemini through Firebase AI Logic. Gemini may use Google Search to answer. If you allowed calendar access, the titles and times of your next seven days of events are included, so it can find a free time. If you are handing off a screen, its text is included too. Suggested items are saved only when you tap Save.
  • The Gemini app. If you connect Day Kit in the Gemini app, Gemini can ask Day Kit to add a task or note, show what's due, or search what you saved, when you @mention Day Kit. Writes are confirmed with you first.
  • The optional Gemma model. If you tap Install in Settings → Assistant, the model file is downloaded from Google's public host on Hugging Face. Nothing about you is sent.

What Google receives is handled under Google's Privacy Policy and the Firebase terms.

Other apps

The DoorDash button opens DoorDash's search with the items on a list Gemini suggested. You choose the store and place any order yourself, in DoorDash, under DoorDash's policies. "Add to calendar" opens your calendar app for you to confirm. Day Kit never buys anything, sends messages or signs in on your behalf.

Deleting your data

Each tool can delete its data from its own settings, and uninstalling Day Kit removes everything from the phone. We never receive a copy of your Day Kit data, so there is nothing to delete on our side. Data sent to Google through "Ask Gemini (cloud)" or the Gemini app is handled under Google's policies.

Children

Day Kit is a general-purpose productivity app and is not directed at children.

Terms of use

  • Day Kit is a hackathon prototype, provided as is, without warranty of any kind.
  • AI suggestions can be wrong. Check each item before you save it, and check anything you order or schedule in another app.
  • Don't rely on Day Kit for emergencies, or for medical, legal or financial decisions.
  • Use of Gemini, Firebase, Google Search and DoorDash is also subject to those services' own terms.
