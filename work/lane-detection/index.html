Real-time Lane Detection

LaneNet in PyTorch, profiled until it ran in real time

A LaneNet encoder-decoder with segmentation and instance-embedding heads, optimized to 150 frames per second.

99% accuracy on TuSimple
150 FPS on an RTX 3080
6.7 ms latency per frame

Overview

Lane detection models are easy to make accurate and hard to make fast. A model that misses the frame budget is useless on the road no matter how accurate it is. This project chased both numbers at once, and stayed small enough to deploy: about 5.2M parameters in a 4.2 MB model.

How it works

  • A LaneNet-style encoder-decoder pairs a binary segmentation head with an instance-embedding head, on an EfficientNet-B0 or MobileNetV2 backbone.
  • DBSCAN clustering separates individual lane instances from the embedding space.
  • Spline fitting and inverse perspective mapping turn raw masks into clean lane geometry.
  • Mixed-precision training (AMP) speeds up the pipeline, and TorchScript export packages the model for deployment.

Results

  • 99% accuracy on the TuSimple benchmark.
  • 150 FPS, roughly 6.7 ms per frame, on an NVIDIA RTX 3080.
  • 80% faster inference and 7% higher accuracy than the prior baseline, F1 0.95 vs 0.85.
  • Compact deployment footprint: ~5.2M parameters, ~4.2 MB model.
