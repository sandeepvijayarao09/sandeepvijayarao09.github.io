Real-time Human Pose Estimation

Published at IEEE AISP 2024

A lightweight keypoint regression head on MediaPipe landmark priors, with an LSTM activity classifier riding on top, all running in real time.

85% mAP, COCO standard
0.78 IoU, multi-person
30+ FPS, real time

Overview

Pose estimation research mostly trades accuracy against compute. This work, published at IEEE AISP 2024 while I was an undergrad, keeps the full pipeline real time: landmark extraction, keypoint refinement, and activity recognition in one pass.

How it works

  • MediaPipe Holistic provides 33 landmark priors per frame, doing the cheap coarse work first.
  • A lightweight keypoint regression head refines those priors; the published model cuts parameter count by 22% while staying within 2% of full-model baselines.
  • 3D joint angles and velocities feed an LSTM classifier covering 11 activities, from locomotion to strength training to yoga.
  • Exercise rep counting and COCO-standard evaluation, mAP, OKS, and IoU, round out the system.

Results

  • 85% mAP and 0.78 IoU on multi-person pose estimation, COCO standard.
  • Real-time inference at 30+ FPS.
  • 11-activity recognition from pose dynamics via LSTM.
  • Published at IEEE AISP 2024.
