SkillGap AI

3x PMI San Francisco Award winner, sole developer

A skills-gap analysis platform that maps user profiles against live job-market embeddings and turns the difference into a learning roadmap.

500+ concurrent users served
70% faster roadmap generation
3 PMI San Francisco awards

Overview

Career advice is usually generic because comparing one person against a whole job market is hard. SkillGap AI does the comparison literally: your profile and live job postings land in the same embedding space, and the gap between them becomes your upskilling plan.

I built and deployed it as sole developer; it won three PMI San Francisco awards in the NextGen Leaders category.

How it works

  • A RAG pipeline maps user profiles against embeddings of live job-market data.
  • Gap analysis surfaces the specific skills standing between a profile and a target role.
  • Roadmap generation turns those gaps into ordered, targeted upskilling recommendations.
  • React frontend, PostgreSQL and Supabase backend, deployed on Vercel.

Results

  • Served 500+ concurrent users in production.
  • Reduced career roadmap generation time by 70%.
  • Won 3 PMI San Francisco awards as sole developer.
