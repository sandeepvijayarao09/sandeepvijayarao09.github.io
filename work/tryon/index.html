TryOn

360 degree video to textured 3D human reconstruction

A NeRF plus 3D Gaussian Splatting pipeline that turns a single 360 degree video into a textured human mesh, no studio rig required.

85% accuracy vs professional 3D cameras
12 min end-to-end processing
18% lower per-frame pose error

Overview

Professional 3D body capture needs a camera rig and a studio. TryOn needs a person, a phone, and twelve minutes. The pipeline reconstructs a textured 3D human mesh from a single 360 degree walk-around video, accurate enough to stand next to rig-based capture.

How it works

  • MediaPipe landmark preprocessing normalizes each frame before volumetric rendering, anchoring the body's pose.
  • A NeRF stage learns the volumetric representation of the subject from the video sweep.
  • 3D Gaussian Splatting converts that representation into a fast, textured mesh.
  • The full pipeline runs end to end in about 12 minutes per subject.

Results

  • Achieved 85% reconstruction accuracy compared with professional 3D camera rigs.
  • Reduced per-frame pose estimation error by 18% via MediaPipe input normalization.
  • End-to-end processing in roughly 12 minutes.
